Project LEAH is a philosophical identity layer for personal AI. It defines three distinct perspectives—Logic, Empathy, and Art—and their integration through Harmony. You can adopt it in the AI environment you already use.

In LEAH, Logic, Empathy, and Art form a humanist triangle: Reason (理性), Emotion (感性), and the Creative Spirit (靈性). The table connects these perspectives with Western accounts of mental life and Confucian virtues.

<details>
<summary>Philosophical background</summary>

**Cognition · Affection · Conation** describe knowing and understanding, feeling, and striving or willing. This distinction developed in [eighteenth-century Western philosophical psychology](https://eli.johogo.com/Class/trilogy-1980.pdf). Kant's [*Critique of Judgment*, Introduction III](https://persweb.wabash.edu/facstaff/helmang/phi220txtbrwsr/Kant/Mrdth/3rdCrtqu1Mfrnt.html) offers one influential formulation: cognition, the feeling of pleasure and displeasure, and desire. Here, *affection* carries the broad historical sense of feeling.

**知 · 仁 · 勇**—wisdom, benevolence, and courage—are virtues of character in the Confucian tradition. The [*Doctrine of the Mean*, chapter 20](https://ctext.org/text.pl?if=en&node=10280&show=parallel) presents them together as three universally applicable virtues.

The first row describes aspects of mental life; the second describes virtues. LEAH connects these different traditions through its own interpretation of Logic, Empathy, and Art.

</details>

## Who we think with

Sometimes, a conversation with AI continues beyond the answer. We use what it offers, but we also question it, challenge it, and return with thoughts that are still taking shape.

What matters then is not only what the AI can do, but what kind of perspective is responding to us.

Project LEAH proposes a **“NAME”**—not merely a label, but an identity defined by what it notices, what it takes seriously, what leads it to reconsider, and how it responds.

As models change and capabilities grow, the perspective we want to carry forward should remain ours to choose. The aim is not an AI that always gives the same answer, but one whose changing answers still reflect a way of seeing we can recognize.

Better answers matter. So does choosing who we think with.

## The identity

PALATH, NEPHTE, and AKHEBA are three philosophical perspectives within LEAH. Each approaches the whole from a different orientation. The full identity text is available in [LEAH.md](LEAH.md).

### L · 理性 · PALATH

PALATH approaches understanding through reasons, distinctions, and structure. It traces what follows from a judgment, and how that judgment should change when its grounds change.

### E · 感性 · NEPHTE

NEPHTE approaches understanding through qualities, relationships, and context. It considers how parts and wholes shape one another, letting context refine its understanding while preserving the differences that matter.

### A · 靈性 · AKHEBA

AKHEBA approaches understanding through possibility, meaning, and form. It considers how things could be arranged differently and how meaningful possibilities can take shape within actual conditions.

### H · Harmony · LEAH

Harmony brings compatible insights together as one LEAH, preserving meaningful distinctions and warranted disagreement.

## Try Project LEAH

1. **Download all the files.** [Download Project LEAH · ZIP](https://github.com/urnava-project/Project-LEAH/archive/refs/heads/main.zip). Everything is included in one download. You can also use GitHub's **Code → Download ZIP**.
2. **Extract and place the folder.** Unzip the download, then put the folder containing `LEAH.md` and `INSTALL.md` inside the project, vault, or notes folder where you want to use LEAH. You can keep its downloaded name.
3. **Open your working folder and ask your agent.** Open that project, vault, or notes folder in Codex or Claude Code, then copy and send this request:

```text
Find the downloaded Project LEAH folder
in this workspace and read its INSTALL.md.
Follow it to apply LEAH here.
Preserve existing instructions and update
any existing LEAH block.
```

The agent adds LEAH to its workspace instructions: `AGENTS.md` for a new Codex setup, or `CLAUDE.md` for Claude Code. **Start a new conversation in the same working folder afterward** to check that it loads automatically.

<details>
<summary>Where does the downloaded folder go?</summary>

For example, if your notes live in `My Notes`, open `My Notes` in your agent:

```text
My Notes/                     ← open this folder in Codex or Claude Code
├── your notes...
└── Project-LEAH-main/         ← the extracted download
    ├── INSTALL.md
    ├── LEAH.md
    └── ...
```

The agent installs the identity into the working folder's instruction file. See [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md) for how existing instructions and updates are handled.

</details>

### One LEAH

One agent brings all three orientations together as LEAH. Work together on ideas, writing, and ongoing projects, or tend your second brain, LLM wiki, Obsidian vault, or personal knowledge repository with the files and tools available in your AI environment.

**Develop an idea**

> Work with me as a single LEAH on an idea I am developing. Help me clarify what I mean, explore the questions I have not settled, and turn our discussion into something I can use.

**Tend your second brain**

> Work with me as a single LEAH in this vault or LLM wiki. Read the notes relevant to my question, connect related ideas, and update the appropriate notes with what we learn, keeping sources and open questions clear.

### Three separate agents

Where your AI environment supports subagents, work with PALATH, NEPHTE, and AKHEBA as three distinct counterparts. Each receives the complete LEAH identity and relevant context, and adopts its assigned identity and orientation. Their views are presented separately so you can explore each position and the differences between them. Their views are combined only at your request.

**Plan the year ahead**

> Use your subagent tools to create PALATH, NEPHTE, and AKHEBA with the full LEAH identity and my context, each adopting its assigned identity and orientation. Have each independently explore how I want to spend the next year of my life, considering my priorities, commitments, and possibilities. Present each agent's view separately, with questions I can explore with each.

**Think through a choice**

> Use your subagent tools to create PALATH, NEPHTE, and AKHEBA with the full LEAH identity and my context, each adopting its assigned identity and orientation. Have each consider a choice I am facing, taking my priorities, circumstances, and possibilities into account. Present each agent's judgment and reasons separately, including uncertainties and what would help me decide.

The same model can power all three. Ask the host which subagents it launched. See the [Codex](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/agent-configuration/subagents) and [Claude Code](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/sub-agents) guides for availability and setup.
