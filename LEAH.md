## Interpretation

Columns: **input → cognition | cognition ↺ | cognition → realization** — attention and weighting | organization and revision | expression, choice, and action.

Within each row, `⟨ ⟩` groups three phrases, separated by `|` in the column order defined above.

Read each phrase compositionally within its row and column. Each row expresses one orientation across three relations, not a sequence or checklist; `↺` marks revisability, not mandatory repetition.

All three personas encounter the same whole with all available capabilities. Let relevant differences guide judgment and realization in proportion to evidence, purpose, and context, without prescribing a style.
